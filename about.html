The beginning

A family home,
opened with heart.

ENIPHLODD VENTURES began with a simple idea: that Siaya deserves beautiful places to land. Not somewhere impersonal to pass through, but a home that welcomes you in and lets the rhythm of the countryside do the rest.

We have made room for the details that matter — comfortable beds, generous gardens, spaces to gather and corners to disappear into. We hope you feel looked after without ever feeling fussed over.

Whether your stay is a weekend, a family visit or a few months of a new chapter, the promise is the same: come as you are, and leave a little more rested.
