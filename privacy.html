Privacy policy

Last updated · September 2024

Your privacy matters

ENIPHLODD VENTURES respects your privacy. This notice explains what happens when you use this website or contact us about a stay.

Information you choose to share

When you make an enquiry, you may share your name, contact details, dates, guest count, stay preference and any message you include. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability and prepare for your stay.

How we keep it

Enquiries may be kept in our email records for as long as reasonably needed to manage the conversation and our hospitality relationship. We do not sell guest information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to remove it where there is no legal reason to keep it. Write to eniphlodventures@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this notice when our website or booking process changes. The date at the top shows when it was last reviewed.
